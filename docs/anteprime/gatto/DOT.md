# Dot — the mascot of imad.chat

The single source of truth for the cat. Read it before touching anything in this folder; keep its last section up to date.
Imad's words are quoted in Italian. Everything he sees must be in Italian.

## 1. Who Dot is (Imad's decisions, do not relitigate)

- "chat" is cat in French: the site's mascot is a **cat called Dot**. It comes out of the dot key of `imad.chat`.
- **Black cat, green eyes, stylised and cute** ("lo voglio stilizzato cute carino, non deve essere un gatto vero"), but **a cat with a real build: separate legs and body**, not a ball ("più gatto con zampe e corpo distinti").
- **2D with depth** ("fallo in 2d ma con profondità"). The first draft in real 3D, made of spheres and cylinders, was rejected ("fa schifo fatto così con sfere cilindri e parallelogrammi"). No visible 3D primitives, ever. No three.js here.
- **A red Moroccan fez** with a tassel. "magari crea anche l'animazione che a volte quando ci clicchi sopra gli cade."
- "altre volte quando clicchi su di lui miagola, se lo svegli si infastidisce e si allontana, tutte queste cose del genere devi pensare e fare"; "devi anche creare molti modi in cui interagirà il gattino nel sito, pensaci tu."
- First time it appears, a tiny bubble: **"salam, sono Dot."** Nothing else in it.
- Only when the visitor has been still for a long time, or the site has been open for a long time, a bubble: **"Lo sapevi che «chat» vuol dire gatto in francese?"**
- The opening he approved ("l'animazione dalle immagini che ho visto mi piace"): the nine keys of `imad.chat` (a 3x3 face, the dot in the centre) fold onto the dot key, the key drops and becomes a box, the lid opens, the cat peeks, looks left and right, jumps out, sits, says who it is. Keys and box are **2D with depth** too.
- "fallo bene per favore, non fare AI slop, animato bene."
- His references (black sticker-like cats: ring or round eyes, a few whisker ticks, lots of character in the poses, cats peeking over a ledge, draped on a ledge, hanging, sliding down with claw marks, long "liquid" cats) are in the session's working folder, never in the repo. He says his friends drew them and allow copying; **we still draw an original cat**: it is his mascot, and a rig that moves cannot be traced from stills anyway. Never reproduce one specific character.
- Our own limits, told to him: Dot **never imitates prayer**; Dot **never stands on, covers or plays over the Corano tile**; Dot is quiet (asleep or away) on the page about the world's conflicts.

## 2. The look

Model units: 1 unit is about 1.5 CSS px on a phone, so Dot sits about 60 px tall and its head is about 35 px wide.

- **Silhouette first.** One near-black shape that reads at a glance in every pose. Big head (wider than tall), short neck, compact body, four short legs that are clearly legs, round paws, a thick expressive tail.
- **Eyes carry the character.** Big, round, green, with a black pupil and one small highlight; the pupils follow the finger. Lids make the mood: wide (startled), half closed and slanted (annoyed), soft arcs (happy, asleep), slow blink (trust).
- **Fez**: red, a short cone with a flat top, a thin black tassel with a bead that swings late. It sits between the ears, tilted a little.
- **Details, few**: two or three thin light whiskers per side; a tiny nose; inner ear in a muted pink or grey; a small pink mouth only when it meows or yawns.
- **Depth without 3D**: far legs and far ear a shade lighter than the body; near limbs overlap the body with a hairline or a soft edge of light; eyes, ears, nose and fez slide across the head when it turns (the rig places them on a sphere); a soft contact shadow on the tile; squash and stretch.
- **Readable on dark and on light**: the site follows the phone's theme and the wallpaper is Torino's sky. On dark, a thin cool edge of light on the top-left of the shapes keeps the black cat visible; on light, none is needed.
- Not wanted: thick outlines, hand-drawn wobble, halftone, emoji faces, gradients that look like plastic, anything that looks like stacked circles.

**The look is `manifesto`** (`look-manifesto.js`): Imad chose it on 2026-11-12 ("manifesto"), over `adesivo` (our pick) and `pelo`; his choice stands. The other two were removed from the repo and the pages on 2026-11-12 (night). `look-base.js` stays only as the plain fallback and test look. What Manifesto is, so whoever touches it keeps it:

- A flat graphic poster cat: near-black shapes, hairline gaps of light (`gap`) where a near limb crosses the body or another limb, far limbs and the far ear a shade lighter (`FAR`), a soft sheen from the top left, tall pointed ears with a muted mauve inside, a long tapered tail with a question-mark curl, ring eyes (green iris, dark rim, black pupil, **one** clear highlight up-left), a small mauve nose, two whiskers per side, the red fez with a black tassel that swings late.
- **The head turned aside is a cat's profile**: the head outline is built from four curves per side; on the side the muzzle points to, every control point blends (by how far the head is turned, `pf`) from the round head to a profile: the forehead slopes, a small stop, a muzzle that sticks out about 2.6 units with the nose at its tip, the chin, the throat. Nose, mouth and whisker roots move to the muzzle; the whiskers of the far side then point forward past the tip. The near ear is redrawn in front of the fez, only beyond a line parallel to the fez axis that slides across the fez as the head turns (nothing covered in front view, all of it in profile): no pop. The far eye is squeezed and then gone past about 0.85 rad; the near eye stays whole. Back views get no muzzle.
- **Forelegs**: the near legs are drawn from the farthest to the nearest (sitting, the hind feet are behind the front ones); a front leg gets a gap line along both its sides from under the shoulder down (two legs, two paws, not a column); a front leg raised in front of the chest gets the gap all around; a raised paw that reaches the lower face (bat, groom, knock) is drawn in front of the head (from Adesivo).
- **Whiskers**: pale on the black face in both themes; outside the head dark on the light theme (seen on the light sky and on white glass) and pale on the dark one; never thinner than about three quarters of a pixel.
- **Edges**: hanging, the hind legs hang a little apart at different heights; sliding on a tile's face all four paws turn their pink pads to us with the toes up and three claws on top, spaced like the three scratch lines of `dot-fx.js` (1.45 units), so the marks start at the claws.
- **Objects**: the paws are drawn over what they hold, only when the object is drawn on the cat's own canvas (held, or `front`; a free object on its own canvas is under the cat anyway): the tea glass and the cube are gripped at their sides (paws half over the edge, two toe cuts toward the centre: Adesivo's grip); over the laptop, the yarn and the star the forearm and paw lie on top.
- **Pad toward the viewer** (`pad` / `pad1` pose keys, the knock): a round paw that grows as it comes closer (`legs[i].fd`), a pink main pad and four toe beans, wider when pressed on the glass.
- **Paws reaching along an edge** (the push poses, `env.pawsOnEdge`): a forearm strip from the shoulder to the paw, above the edge; at rest it has no length, so the plain peek is unchanged.
- **The night rim**: `draw(ctx, frame, { ..., rim: "notte" })` (or `rim: true`) draws the cat for a near-black background (the intro's sky, about `#05060a`): a thin cool line of light only on the edges that face up (ears, head, back, the upper side of the tail, the top arc of paws resting on a ledge): the silhouette drawn 0.9 px higher (and a little to the left) minus the silhouette, so it is full on top, thins to nothing on vertical sides and is absent underneath; never an outline all round (2026-11-13: the old one read as a die-cut sticker); whiskers at alpha 0.55; the body itself stays black and the green eyes and the red fez carry it. `rim: false` = no rim at all; no `rim` = as before (dark theme: a rim up-left; light theme: none). Seen on the sheet `tavola.html?bg=notte` (that background plus `rim=notte`; `?rim=notte|no` alone works on any background).

## 3. What Dot does

Tiers: **P1** is the first preview, **P2** next, **P3** later on the real site.

Everyday life (P1): sits on a tile's top edge; walks along it; jumps between tiles; sits and looks around; loafs; sleeps (slow breathing, fez sliding over one eye); wakes with a stretch and a yawn; blinks; ears twitch; tail always alive.

Edges (from his references): **peeks from behind a tile's top edge** (ears, eyes, two paws on the edge), then climbs up (P1); **lies draped over the edge**, a front leg, a hind leg and the tail hanging down over the tile (P1); **hangs from a tile's lower edge** by the front paws, swinging (P2); **slides down the face of a tile leaving claw marks** that fade, eyes wide, when dropped there (P1).

Touch:
- **Tap**: never the same reaction twice in a row. It meows (mouth opens, a tiny "miao", a sound); or the **fez falls off**: it pops up, lands beside the cat, rolls a little, Dot looks at it, walks over and puts it back on; or a happy hop; or a purr with closed eyes (P1).
- **Tap while asleep**: it wakes **annoyed** (ears flat, half-lidded glare, tail thumping), gets up, turns its back, walks away to another tile and sits facing away for a while; later it looks back and forgives (P1).
- **Many taps in a row**: fed up, it walks off and hides in its box; two green eyes in the dark (P2).
- **Finger held still on it**: petting; it leans in, purrs, slow-blinks; after about 3.4 s it **melts flat** under the finger (body spread, chin on the ground, eyes two happy arcs, slow breath, the tail tip flicking slowly, the fez stays on); when the finger leaves it gets up with a stretch. (The old "rolls belly up" read as a dark blob in all three looks and was dropped on 2026-11-12.)
- **Drag**: picked up by the scruff it becomes a **long cat** (the body stretches), legs limp, tail swinging, an unimpressed look. **Drop** on an edge: it lands and shakes; on a tile's face: it slides with claws; thrown: it flies, lands, tumbles (P1).
- **A finger moving fast nearby**: crouch, rear wiggle, pounce (P2).
- **Dragged into the box and the lid closed**: Dot stays inside until the box is tapped. This is the visitor's off switch (P2).

The page:
- Pupils and head follow the finger or the mouse (P1). With no finger, it looks at the visitor, with glances aside.
- Idle visitor: after about 25 s Dot grooms, yawns, loafs or sleeps; after about 90 s still, or 4 minutes open, once per visit, the French bubble (P1).
- Scrolling: Dot stays attached to its tile; if it ends up off screen it comes back by peeking from a visible tile or running in (P1). A fast scroll makes it brace, ears back (P2).
- It gets out of the way: if the visitor taps the tile under it, it hops aside (P2).
- Site things (P3): bats at the blinking cursor of `imad.chat_`; watches the globe turn; follows a greeting-star with its eyes; at night in Torino it sleeps more; on the tools pages it sits in a corner and watches.
- Reduced motion: Dot sits still and only blinks. Hidden tab: everything pauses.

On the new home (`CASA.md` 2c E; built 2026-11-12 night; how it is mounted: section 5, "Perches"):
- **It never covers words or controls, nor the Corano, nor the quiet tile** while it lives on the tiles: the home passes its whole keep list. Words scroll behind it only while it rides the dock (see below).
- **Three perches are always good**: the hero ledge (home, with the box), the dock (the top of the filter pill, fixed to the screen) and the 3x3 logo of the footer (sitting on its top row of keys, pose `ride`). Tile edges stay perches where the keep test leaves room.
- **It follows the visitor**: out of view 2.4 s with the page still (3.6 before 2026-11-13), it comes back: home if the ledge is in view (from behind the hero's edge, or out of its box), the footer logo if it is in view, else the dock, where it comes up from behind the pill: ears and fez, eyes, two paws on the edge, a look at the visitor and a blink, it gathers and hops on (only where neither the climb nor the seated cat covers a word; elsewhere it stays peeking, see below). It leaves the same way, sinking behind the pill. While the page scrolls, out of view for 0.8 s, it comes up to peek from the dock (words may pass behind it only then). Back at the top and still for five seconds: it goes home. A perch where a come-up went wrong (a word over the edge, the logo's seated cat failing the keep test) is skipped for a minute; before a come-up the seated outline is tested at that spot.
- **On the dock it is calm**: sits compact (`ride`, the tail wrapped forward, nothing hanging over the filters), loafs, sleeps, strolls along the pill, grooms, yawns. While the page scrolls it watches what passes (the pupils follow a point of the page, the head leans a little). A filter tapped: it looks down at it. The Corano or the quiet tile about to pass behind it: it ducks behind the pill at once and comes back up when its place is clear. **When the page has been still for 0.6 s, it covers no word and no control** (CASA.md 2c E.1, decided again by the art director on 2026-11-13): two steps along the pill (26 px at most, the whole path clear) to where the seated cat (or the loaf) covers nothing; farther than that it goes there behind the pill; if no seated spot is clean, it slips down behind the pill and **peeks** (state `dockpeek`), at the highest of three heights that covers nothing: eyes and two paws on the edge, eyes only (narrower), or only the ear tips and the fez; if none fits anywhere on the pill, it ducks fully and waits for the page to move. Peeking clean, it rises to a higher peek, or climbs on, after a while when that spot is clean. A tap on the peeking cat: a "mrr", or it hops up if it may sit there. Its touch target never lies over a control or a filter, so taps reach them; a control counts with its whole box, not only its text.
- **The page carries it against the dock** (scrolling up while it sits on a tile edge just above): it steps onto the pill and turns to look at the tile going away. A filter tapped while it sits on a tile that will travel: it ducks behind that edge at once and comes back later.
- **The bubble is part of Dot** (2026-11-13; on every page, not only the home): it does not open over a word, a control, the Corano, the quiet tile or the dock when there is a free place next to Dot. Its usual place first (above the head, or under the feet when there is no room above), drawn as always when it is free; otherwise above the fez as long as 4 px are left to the top of the window; otherwise beside the head, on the side with more room, then on the other side, the tail on the bubble's side pointing at Dot's temple. Over Dot's own box only when no other place is free; when nothing at all is free it stays in its usual place (the cases are in section 6, same date). On a first visit on a phone "salam, sono Dot." opens above the fez, between the logo and the sound button, and the hero's dateline stays readable; on a computer it opens beside the head, in the sky.

The hint that Dot can be touched (`CASA.md` 2c C; built 2026-11-13; only with `DOT.start({ hint: true })`, which the new home passes; never with reduced motion; no word, nothing away from Dot, all gone in seconds):
- **Near**: a finger or a pointer that comes within about 150 px of its head (while it sits, looks, loafs, thinks or peeks from the dock): ears up and forward, head and pupils to it, pupils wide and round (`pupilT` up to 1.3; the look draws a pupil above 1 rounder and larger); while it is near the eyes stay open: no slow blink, no lid, the next blink pushed 1.5 s ahead, and only one quick blink (110 ms) after 1.2 s; when it goes, Dot stops looking at that spot.
- **The bat**: a finger scrolling past close (or the mouse resting near while the wheel scrolls) gets a quick swat: big eyes, the paw jumps to the glass toward the finger with its pad showing and comes back; from behind, a quick turn of the head instead. At most once per scroll gesture, about six times in ten, never twice within 16 s.
- **The ghost fingertip, once**: about four seconds after "salam, sono Dot." has gone (8 s after a later page load), if nobody has touched Dot: a translucent fingertip (24 px, like the touch dot of a screen recording) comes in on an arc from behind, presses on the top of the back of the head, between the back ear and the fez, with only about its lower third on the cat (a ring; the pad flattens to 1.08 x 0.88 with a soft contact shadow; never over an eye), lifts Dot by the scruff (pose `scruff`: long, loose, half-lidded, unimpressed; the paws leave the edge, they never sink into it, the tail lets itself down only as far as there is room), holds it half a second (a finger is never quite still), puts it down (it lands softly and shakes), lets go (a ring) and leaves; Dot looks where it went, then at the visitor with a slow blink. About 2.7 s. It is a real drag (the state `held` with a fake finger), so a real finger at any instant takes the cat from where it is, with no jump. It waits for a still page (1.2 s), no typing, no sheet, Dot sitting in full view with room above it and nothing of the Corano, of a word or of a control under the cat's outline at the top of the lift plus 4 px (measured: 40 px higher than seated, 13 px wider on the left and 9 on the right), on the dock too (2026-11-13; before, the dock tested controls only). If the page scrolls or a key is typed while Dot is lifted, the fingertip puts it down quickly (0.22 s) and leaves; it never lets it fall across the words. Never again once the visitor has dragged Dot (remembered on the device), once per session, in two sessions at most. On tablet and desktop the hero ledge has too little sky above Dot, so there it waits for another perch (the dock, the logo, a tile edge).
- **The knock**: if still nobody has touched it, the page has been still for 7 s and Dot sits calm and in view: it comes toward the glass (steps in place, 10% larger), sits, raises a paw with the pad toward the visitor and knocks twice on the inside of the screen (a small ring spreads at each knock, no sound), tilts its head one way then the other and waits with big pupils, blinks, then gives up with a small sigh and loafs. Twice per session at most, 45 s apart, 18 s after the ghost fingertip at the earliest (or 40 s after the fingertip was due if it found no place).
- **The first real touch of a session** gets the warmest answer: a happy hop, then a purr with closed eyes (only the purr where a word is right above it).

**The yarn threads** (Imad: "un filo a cui si può appendere il gatto [...] come se tirasse via strappasse il pulsante"; built 2026-11-14 on `window.FILO`, CASA.md 2f H; only with `DOT.start({ filo: true })`, the home):
- **Hung by the visitor**: carried near a thread, Dot looks at it and reaches up with both forepaws; let go within 28 px of the thread (below the knot), it grabs it instead of falling: forepaws one above the other around the wool, above the fez, the head tilted (the fez leans aside, never under the paws), the hind legs tucked, the tail hanging. Feet on the ledge (phones: the knot is 68 px above it, so this comes fast): it plants its hind legs wide, looks up at the button, gives a small tug (the button sinks in its socket), relaxes, gathers up, and yanks: the button tears off. Nothing under the thread (computers: the threads hang beyond the hero): it slides to the end, dangles looking down, draws its legs up and yanks.

On a computer it falls to the tile below and looks up at the button springing back. The tear never toggles the sound or the theme (filo.js).
- **On its own**, now and then: awake and idle on the hero ledge, no opening, no menu, section or sheet, no finger on the threads, the thread still and in view, no reduced motion, at most once every 3 minutes (the first chance about 45 s after the page opens), never twice in a row on the same thread. It walks (or trots) to the end of its part of the ledge nearest the thread, wiggles looking up, coils (220 ms) and leaps to the thread, grabbing it at the top of the arc (8 to 14 px under the knot), and the rest as above. Only where the ledge is under the thread (phones); not on computers.
- **Taken back**: grabbed while it hangs, it lets go into the visitor's hold where it is (no jump). A gentle tap: it lets go and lands. A finger that takes the thread above its paws: it slips off at once and lands (or falls).
- **A thread pulled by a finger**: Dot watches the swinging end; if the end swings past within reach (12 to 64 px aside, up to 72 px above its feet), it bats it once (`FILO.bat`), never while the thread is held.
- While it hangs: no bubble; its touch target starts at the paws (the thread above them and the buttons stay the finger's); nothing of it reaches the Corano (the threads are at the top of the page). `DOT.pause()` lets go of the thread first.
- **On a computer** the pointer is an open hand over Dot (`grab`) and a closed one on the whole page while it is held (`grabbing`).

## 4. The quality bar for the motion

- Anticipation before every jump or pounce; squash on landing, settle after; nothing starts or stops dead.
- Arcs, not straight lines; ease in and out; hold the key poses long enough to read.
- Overlap and follow-through: tail, ears, whiskers, the fez and its tassel arrive late and overshoot.
- Feet never slide while walking; the body bobs twice per cycle; the head stays steadier than the body.
- Every state change is blended; no popping when legs change side or the tail wraps.
- Personality in the timing: curious (quick head, slow body), lazy (long holds), proud of its fez.
- It must run at 60 fps on a phone and cost nothing when off screen or hidden.

Check motion with frame strips (8 to 16 frames of one action side by side), not with single pictures.

## 5. How it is built

Plain scripts, no library, no build step. **ES2017 at most** (no optional chaining, no `??`, no class fields, no modules): the page must run on older iPhones. Imad's phone has iOS 18.0. Nothing here can test Safari: say so.

Files in `docs/anteprime/gatto/` (each has one owner while agents work in parallel):

- `dot-rig.js` — the skeleton. `DOT.makeRig(preset)` gives a rig: poses (`DOT.POSES`), proportions (`DOT.PROPS`), a pseudo-3D pose (body yaw, pitch, roll; head yaw, pitch, roll) projected to 2D. `rig.step(dt)` smooths toward the target pose; `rig.frame()` returns the **frame**: plain 2D geometry in model units, origin at the ground point under the cat, x right, **y up**. The frame is the contract between movement and drawing: fields may be added, never renamed or removed. Two controls added on 2026-11-12: `rig.headMax` (how far, in radians, the head may turn away from the viewer; soft limit, `null` = none) and `rig.breathRate` (radians per second, default 1.9; changing it never jumps).
- `look-*.js` — a look registers `DOT.looks[name] = { label, preset, draw(ctx, frame, env), drawFez(ctx, fez, env), drawProp(ctx, name, p, env) }` (`drawProp` draws Dot's objects: `tea`, `cube`, `yarn`, `star`, `laptop`; origin at the object's base, y up). `ctx` arrives already transformed to model units with y up. A look never moves anything: it only draws the frame. Its `preset` may override proportions and pose numbers.
- `dot-brain.js` — behaviour: states, timing, physics, touch, bubbles, scratches, the loose fez, sounds. It talks to the rig by pose name, overrides and a few controls. It may register extra poses of its own; it does not edit the rig.
- `dot-stage.js` — the nine keys and the box, Canvas 2D.
- `dot-sound.js`, `media/` — sounds.
- `index.html`, `gatto.css` — the preview page: mock tiles, dark and light, sound, "Rivedi" (no look switch any more: it opens on Manifesto, `?look=base` gives the test look).
- `dot-fx.js` — the small things around Dot: the bubble, the scratches, the canvases of loose objects (fez, cube, yarn, star). The bubble chooses its own place: `Bubble.place(ax, ay, fy, vx0, vy0, vw, vis, o)`, where `o` is the brain's `FUM` (`ok(l, t, r, b, margin, lax)` says whether a rectangle is free, `hy` and `hr` say where the side of the head is); without `o` it is placed as before 2026-11-13.
- `tavola.html`, `prova.html`, `scena.html`, `riordina.html`, `strumenti/` — tools for checking, never online (see section 6).

Drawing surfaces: canvases **positioned absolutely in page coordinates** inside one layer above the tiles, so they scroll with the page natively (a fixed full-screen canvas lags behind iOS scrolling). One small canvas follows the cat; the box and keys have their own; the loose fez and the scratches get theirs when needed. All of them `pointer-events: none`; one small transparent button over the cat takes the touches (`touch-action: none` on it only). "Behind a tile" is done by clipping the cat at the tile's edge.

Tiles tell Dot what they are with `data-dot`: `no` (never on it or over it: the Corano), `quiet` (stay away and still). Details in "How to mount Dot on a page" below.

### How to mount Dot on a page

**Markup.** The page needs the tiles (any elements matching one selector), a home tile for the box, and one empty clipping layer for Dot:

```html
<body style="position: relative">                      <!-- the clip layer below covers the whole page, not only the first screen -->
  <div class="dot-clip" id="dotclip"></div>              <!-- position:absolute; top:0; right:0; bottom:0; left:0; overflow:hidden; pointer-events:none; z-index under every sheet -->
  ... <div class="t" data-home>hero</div>
      <div class="t" data-dot="no">Corano</div>
      <div class="t" data-dot="quiet">Nel mondo</div>
      <div class="t">...</div> ...
</body>
```

Without the clipping parent the cat's canvas sticks out on the right near the screen edge and the phone widens the page (seen in the tests: `innerHeight` grew from 844 to 896). Write `top/right/bottom/left` next to any `inset` for older Safari.

**`data-dot` on a tile:**
- none: an ordinary tile. Dot sits, walks, sleeps, plays on its top edge, slides down its face, hangs from its bottom edge. Tiles smaller than 40 x 24 px and hidden tiles (`display: none`) are ignored; the corner radius is read once per full refresh so Dot keeps off the rounded corners.
- `no` (the Corano): Dot never stands on it, covers it or plays over it. Its own top edge is never used, and the part of any edge less than 84 px under it (Dot's height with the fez) is cut away. Jumps, throws, the loose fez and the objects never cross it (checked on the true arc, whole body). Dropped in front of it, or with any part of it in front of it at the moment of release, Dot leaps away at once. Only while the visitor drags it can it pass over it.
- `quiet` (the page about the world's conflicts): strict. Dot never chooses its edge nor an edge right under it (these are kept, but flagged); if it lands there (dropped, thrown, falling) it leaves at once with a quick jump: no shake, no bubble, no game, no sound; a tap there only hurries it away; dropped on its face it leaps away (no scratches, no hanging); no object, game, pounce, petting or bubble starts while any part of Dot is over it or within about 150 px; objects never rest on it. Jumping past in front of it is allowed. If no other edge is visible, Dot hides behind the edge and waits out of sight.
- `data-home` (or `home:` in the options): the tile whose top edge holds the box (at 58% of its width if wider than 220 px, else in the middle; `homeX` changes the 58%). It must have headroom: no `no` tile right above it (if there is one, the opening is skipped).

- `perch` (the home's footer 3x3 logo): only its top edge is used, sitting compact (pose `ride`); nobody slides down its face or hangs from it.

**`data-dot-keep` on any element** (or a selector in `DOT.start({ keep })`): words Dot must never sit in front of (titles, lines of text, chips, a chart). Dot reads where the text really is (the line boxes of the text, so a short title in a wide block only blocks its own piece; text that overflows its box, like a scrolling row of greetings, blocks the whole box). For the edge under it:
- a word less than 52 px above the edge (at phone size; it scales with Dot): that part of the edge is flagged like a quiet one: Dot never chooses it; dropped there it walks to the nearest clear part of the same edge, or jumps away;
- a word 52 to 74 px above: Dot may only **lie** there (loaf, sleep): it arrives with a jump and crouches at once, never sits, stretches, shakes or yawns there; a tap sends it to an edge where it can sit (there it meows and plays); no petting, bubble or game there;
- 74 px or more: free.
The margin on each side of a word is about 46 px (Dot's half-width with the tail). A guard checks Dot's real outline five times a second while it rests: if any part of it is in front of a word or of the Corano (a layout change, a landing at the end of an edge), it walks to the nearest clear spot of the same edge, or jumps away. **On a phone, with words at the bottom of almost every tile and 12 px gaps, this leaves very few edges** (see section 6).

**Controls** (inside the keep list: links, buttons, fields, anything with `tabindex`, `.pill`, `.qr__carta`) count more than words: Dot riding the dock never stays at rest over one (it steps aside, or ducks behind the pill until the page moves), because a tap there would go to the cat.

**Perches, on a page with `dock` (`strict`)** (CASA.md 2c E). The keep list stays whole, so on a phone few tile edges are left; three perches are always good:
- **the home ledge** (the home tile's top edge, with the box);
- **the dock**: its top edge, read again at every frame (page coordinates = scroll + its place on the screen). While Dot is on it its canvas, the touch target and the bubble live in the fixed layer and are placed in screen coordinates, so the cat rides with the screen and does not tremble while the page scrolls (that is the point of the fixed layer: a canvas in page coordinates moved by script lags behind iOS's own scrolling). The cat is drawn above the dock (z 51 over 50), nothing below the top of the pill is drawn (a clip at the top edge + 1.5 px: the tail and paws never cover a filter's word), the contact shadow falls on the pill; the touch target is trimmed so it never reaches into the dock, so a tap on a filter always reaches the filter;
- **perch tiles** (`data-dot="perch"`: the footer logo).

Ordinary tile edges stay perches where the keep test allows them. **Travel** in strict mode never passes in front of a word, a control, the quiet tile or behind the dock: every jump is checked on its true arc with a body box (`arcKeep`), the come-back never jumps in from off screen (it peeks from behind an edge, comes out of the box or comes up from behind the dock), a spot that becomes bad is left by two steps or by ducking behind the edge (never a fall across the words below), the fez toss, the star and the knocked cube start only where their path is clear. **The follow rule** (`follow2`, every frame): out of view about `followAfter` seconds with the page still for half a second (or 2.5 s more while it scrolls), Dot comes back: to the home ledge if it is in view (from behind the hero's edge, or out of its box), else the footer logo, else a free tile edge in the middle of the screen, else the dock (it comes up from behind the pill: ears and fez, eyes, paws, a look, a hop on: state `dockup`); it leaves the same way (`dockdown`). Back at the top and still for 3 s: it goes home. On the dock: it sits (pose `ride`), loafs, sleeps, walks along the pill, grooms; while the page scrolls it watches what passes (the pupils follow a point of the page, a small lean); a filter tapped: it looks down at it; the Corano or the quiet tile coming behind it: it ducks (`ducked`) and comes up when its place is clear; at rest with words behind it, it walks along the pill to a clear place (or loafs where a lower cat fits), with a control behind it and no clear place, it ducks. A tile edge it sits on that the scroll brings against the dock: it steps onto the dock (`board`). A filter tapped while it sits on a tile that is about to travel: it ducks behind the edge at once and comes back later. Taps work on the dock (meow, hop, purr; no fez toss there), a drag takes it off the dock.

**Calls:**
- `DOT.refresh()`: re-read the tiles after a layout change (tiles reordered by a filter, resized, shown or hidden). Dot stays with its tile, at the same place on it; a jump in flight lands where its target is now (a target that moved more than 140 px is not chased: Dot drops from where it is, or jumps clear); the box follows the home tile; scratches, the loose fez and the objects move with their tiles. If Dot's edge is gone, or its spot is now under the Corano or on or under the quiet tile, it moves to a good visible edge (falls to the edge below, walks a few steps, or jumps). `DOT.refresh(seconds)`: also follow the tiles at every frame for that long, so Dot rides its tile during an animation. For a FLIP animation call `DOT.refresh(duration + 0.05)` right after setting the inverted transforms (tested: the cat stays exactly on its tile in every frame of a 450 ms animation). Window resizes and rotations are handled by Dot itself.
- `DOT.pause()` / `DOT.resume()`: freeze everything while a sheet covers the page: no frames (the animation loop stops), no sounds (the purr stops), the bubble and the touch target are hidden, touches are ignored, Dot's clock stops (so no timer runs on: the French bubble, sleep, moods). `resume()` re-reads the tiles first. `DOT.paused()` tells which.
- `DOT.setLook(name)`, `DOT.setDark(bool)`, `DOT.say(text, seconds)`, `DOT.replay()` (the opening again), `DOT.stop()` (removes everything).
- Sound: `DOT.sound.setMuted(bool)` (the site's sound button).
- `DOT.debug` is for the tools only (manual clock with `window.__manual = true` and `DOT.debug.tick(dt)`, `press()`/`lift()` for a finger held still, `refresh`, `quiet()`, `fezInfo()`, `bbox()`, ...). For the hint: `hint()` (its state: phase of the fingertip, knocks, memory), `hintGo()` (the fingertip now, if Dot sits on an edge), `hintCan()` (whether the real conditions allow it now), `hintKnock()`, `hintAt(s)`, `hintReset()` (forgets session and device), `hintGeo()` (eyes, lowest paw, lowest point of the tail, the edge, the fingertip, in page pixels). For the yarn: `filo(id)` (the gag on its own now, if Dot is at home and a thread is reachable: true), `filoPlan()` (where it would stand), `hangRope(id, s, vx, vy)` (hung now, as if let go there), `rope()` (state, thread, s, feet), `ropeNext(s)` (the next gag on its own in s seconds).

**Layers.** Dot's layer has z-index 40 inside its parent; sheets and panels must be above the parent, and call `DOT.pause()` when they cover the page. Dot's canvases are drawn at the screen's real pixel ratio up to 3.
